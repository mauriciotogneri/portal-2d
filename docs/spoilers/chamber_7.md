## Entry

Run 3, chamber 2, combine, and the run's last chamber. Elements: two boxes, two buttons, a door both link, two antlines; walls, steel and the exit are known from run 1. The player knows runs 1 and 2, and 3.1 (turning in place). It should teach that a door several buttons link opens only while all of them are held, each antline lighting as its own button goes down, and that a box bars the tile it rests on. Hint: none.

## Layout

8 × 5. A 2 × 3 room on the left, where the subject starts in the middle of its left column, facing right, a box above it and a box below. From the middle of the room's right column, **R** (2, 2), a one-tile corridor runs right along the chamber's middle row: the near button **N** (3, 2), the far button **F** (4, 2), the tile beyond them **T** (5, 2), the door (6, 2), and the exit right behind it, in the chamber's right wall. Both buttons link the door. Every wall is steel but the antlines' walls: N's antline runs up from N, along the top wall and down into the door; F's runs down from F, along the bottom wall and up into the door. The wall tiles they run over are plain, so their dots show, but none of those faces a floor tile, so none takes a portal; the one tile right beside each button, which does face one, is steel, and hides its line there.

## Intended route

1. Face up (up, into the box) and pick up box 0. Walk right over R and N onto F; interact: box 0 goes down on T. (F's line lights while the subject stands there.)
2. Walk back left to the start, face down (down, into box 1) and pick it up. Walk right onto F again, facing right, box 0 ahead on T.
3. Turn left in place; interact: box 1 goes down on N. Both buttons are held (N by box 1, F by the subject): both lines light and the door opens.
4. Face right (right, into box 0) and pick it up. Step right onto T: F comes up and the door shuts.
5. Turn left in place; interact: box 0 goes down on F. Both lines light and the door opens for good.
6. Walk right through the door and out through the exit.

Either box can go first (the state walk reaches two completed states, one per order), and box 1 can be fetched from R's column as well. Solver: 12 moves and 0 portals, both the fewest; the state walk's fewest presses (23) are this route. Every completing route turns in place at least twice (the walk's fewest turn actions on a completing route: 2).

## Lure and where the player should get stuck

The subject starts facing the corridor, the two buttons one and two tiles beyond R, with both antlines in view running round into the one door. The first plan is chamber 1.3's: pick up a box, step to R, put it down on N. N's line lights and the door stays shut, F's line unlit: the door needs both, and the box now bars the corridor (guideline 67). That is the first lure, plain at once (guideline 4), and picking the box up again undoes it.

The second is chamber 3.1's answer, which this chamber is built to invite: carry a box past a button and put it down on it from beyond. On F, from T: F's line lights, the door stays shut, and the subject is past both buttons with the second box in the room, shut out by the first. On N, from F: the door opens while the subject stands on F, and shuts on the step off, with the second box still in the room. Each fails as plainly, undone by picking the box up again, and each fails for the same reason, the order, which is the catch.

A third: the first box on F from N, the subject on N facing right. The door opens, both lines lit, but the box on F bars the way and the step off N shuts it again.

Where the player should get stuck: with one box in hand and the other in the room, seeing that a box can be left beyond both buttons, on T, and come back for later from F, once the second box is down.

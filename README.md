# Portal 2D

A grid-based puzzle game played in the browser, a fan-made 2D take on Valve's Portal. Guide a test subject across test chambers to the exit, using a pair of portals to get past walls, gaps and obstacles. There are no enemies and no way to die: each chamber is a puzzle of where to place portals, what to carry where, and in which order to open things.

Play it at [portal2d.com](https://portal2d.com).

## How to play

- Nothing moves on its own: the chamber changes only when you act, one action at a time.
- A move key turns the subject to face that way. Press it again to step forward.
- Portals are shot the way the subject faces and open on the first wall the shot reaches. Walk into one portal to come out of the other.
- Carry boxes, press buttons and use switches to open the way, then reach the open exit to complete the chamber.
